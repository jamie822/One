# Live-hero recipes

Every client gets a BUILT, MOVING scene occupying the majority of the hero
(the founder's floor — see `research/founder-taste.md`). Each recipe is
used for AT MOST ONE client, then marked. The design-craft doctrine agent
contributes further recipes to this file; keep them one paragraph each and
concrete enough to build from.

## Shipped

1. **The Power Core** — USED: ES Elec (Leeds). Dark warm charcoal world;
   glowing amber orb carrying the brand mark inside three concentric rings
   with orbiting sparks; constellation canvas wiring itself across the
   hero; breathing aurora glows; faint engineer's grid; trust marquee
   closing the frame. Tech: CSS keyframes + one rAF canvas, all
   transform/opacity.

2. **The Rising Sun** — USED: Loxley Solar (Sheffield). Warm daylight
   world; a large sun with a soft scalloped corona (conic-gradient ray
   ring under a radial mask, slow rotation) rising out of the left
   horizon, crossing high and setting behind a drawn roofline carrying
   tilted panels; twinkling glint canvas; the interactive yield estimator
   floating on the scene as a white instrument. Tech: CSS keyframes
   (translateX wrapper + eased translateY disc) + one rAF canvas.

7. **The Day the Panels Work** — time-of-day simulation: the whole hero's

8. **The Van at First Light** — drawn (not photographic) flat-illustration

## 17. The Street Comes On — SHIPPED (hero lab, 2026-07-29)

The trade's whole promise in four seconds. A Leeds terrace street at dusk,
dark. A pulse of current runs the overhead cable (SMIL bead + dash-reveal
brightening of the wire); the streetlamp flickers awake (steps() flicker,
halo + light cone); every window ignites in a wave timed to the pulse's
x-position over each house. Then the street LIVES: clouds drift, canvas
stars breathe, one window flickers with telly-glow, a random light toggles
every ~10s as people move rooms, pointer parallax on three depth layers.
INTERACTIVE: every window is a button (click toggles it); a master switch
re-runs the whole choreography. Houses generated by a SEEDED PRNG so the
composition is art-directed, not random per load. Pause control (WCAG
2.2.2); reduced motion = street fully lit, static stars (designed still).
No photography, no libraries; one canvas; ~104KB total with three inline
brand fonts. Source: clients/es-elec/design-system/hero-lab-street.html.
Demo: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/37a82416-9b9e-4e40-86c7-28e77b4a4065

## 18. The Filament — SHIPPED (hero lab, 2026-07-29)

Tungsten's namesake as the hero: a giant filament coil drawn live with
light on one canvas — four stroke passes (ultra-wide bloom to white-hot
core) on the true colour ramp of heated tungsten. ARRIVAL SURGE: powers
to near-full on load then settles to a breathing ember. THE INSTRUMENT:
a real dimmer slider drives one temperature model (colour, bloom radius,
ambient pool, spark rate, motes all follow); cursor proximity flares the
nearest turns and spits spark streaks. Support stems ground the coil.
Pause control; reduced-motion = steady warm filament still. ~97KB total,
zero libraries. Source: clients/es-elec/design-system/hero-lab-filament.html
Demo: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/37a82416-9b9e-4e40-86c7-28e77b4a4065
