# Client intake — send this, get a website

Fifteen minutes of the client's time. Every unanswered question becomes a
plausible temporary value on the demo site, clearly flagged, swapped before
go-live — so partial answers never block a build.

## The business
1. Trading name, and the legal name if different
2. Who answers the phone? (name, and are they on the tools?)
3. Year you started trading; roughly how many jobs or installs to date
4. Base town + the areas you actually cover (list every town you want work from)
5. Phone number, email, business address (street address stays private if
   you are service-area only — say which)
6. Opening hours; do you take emergency call-outs, and when?

## The work
7. List your services, in the order you WANT more of them
8. For each: a rough "from" price you are happy to publish (or "don't publish")
9. Which services carry your certifications? (NICEIC / NAPIT / MCS / other,
   with registration numbers — we link the public register)
10. Insurance cover level; any workmanship guarantee you offer

## Proof
11. Google Business Profile link (or tell us if you don't have one — setting
    it up is part of go-live)
12. Links to reviews anywhere (Google, Checkatrade, Trustpilot, Which?)
13. 5-15 real photos: you, the van, finished jobs, before/after (phone
    photos are fine; we grade them)
14. Any logo / brand colours, or "build me one"

## The sell
15. Finish this sentence: "People choose me instead of the bigger firms
    because ______"
16. The one job type you'd happily do every day if the phone let you
17. What do customers say when they recommend you? (their words, not yours)
18. Response promise you can actually keep (e.g. "call back within the hour,
    weekdays")

## Practical
19. Existing website/domain? (we need registrar access at go-live)
20. Anything you refuse to do / areas you won't travel to / jobs to filter out
