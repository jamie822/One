## POST `/blog/rewire-cost-leeds/`

Title: How Much Does a Rewire Cost in Leeds? 2026 Prices
Meta: A full rewire in Leeds costs £3,200 to £8,500 in 2026 depending on size.
A Leeds electrician breaks down real prices per house type and what moves them.

H1: How much does a rewire cost in Leeds? 2026 prices

Lead (direct answer, GEO-quotable): A full house rewire in Leeds costs between
£3,200 and £8,500 in 2026 (TEMP range). A 2-bed terrace sits at the bottom of
that range, a 5-bed detached at the top. Partial rewires run £1,200 to £2,800
(TEMP). Those are working figures from jobs quoted this year, not national
averages: Leeds labour rates run under London by a good margin, and most
of the housing stock here is terraces and semis that wire predictably.

### The table (per house type, all TEMP)
- 2-bed terrace: £3,200 to £4,200 · 5 to 6 days
- 3-bed semi: £4,000 to £5,500 · 6 to 8 days
- 4-bed detached: £5,500 to £7,000 · 8 to 10 days
- 5-bed detached: £7,000 to £8,500 · 10 to 12 days
Caption: Full rewire, materials and labour, certificate and notification
included. Your written quote is the real number; this table is for orientation.

### What moves the price
Walls and floors first: solid floors and lath-and-plaster walls slow the
chasing right down. Then how much you want moved rather than replaced like for
like. Then the finish: brushed steel sockets across a whole house add hundreds
on their own. Occupied houses go slower than empty ones because putting your
rooms back together every evening is real work. Nobody itemises that last one,
which is why two quotes for the same house can sit £1,000 apart.

### Do you need a full rewire at all?
Probably not, if the wiring is PVC and the problems are localised. A house
fails on specifics, not on age. An EICR (£140, half a morning, TEMP) tells you
what state yours is in before you commit to anything. I have talked more than
one caller out of a rewire after the inspection came back cleaner than they
feared. Rubber or fabric-insulated cable, though, or a fuse box with a wooden
back: stop reading and book the inspection this week.

### How to read a rewire quote
A real quote names the number of circuits, says whether chasing and making
good are included, includes the certificate and Building Control notification,
and states what happens when something unexpected turns up behind a wall. If
any of those four is missing, the price is not finished. The cheapest of three
quotes is usually the one that grows.

### What happens on the job
Day one is protection and first fix upstairs. Power stays on overnight, every
night. Kitchens and bathrooms get planned around your mornings. Last day is
testing, every circuit, and you get the EIC certificate before the van leaves
the street.
