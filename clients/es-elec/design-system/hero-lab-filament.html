What you're looking at

A tungsten filament, drawn with light. The coil is rendered live on canvas in three passes — wide bloom, inner glow, white-hot core — with the colour ramp of real heated tungsten: ember red through amber to blazing white. It breathes at idle, and micro-flicker keeps it organic, never static.

The dimmer is real. Drag it and the coil heats: colour, bloom radius, background warmth, spark rate and the dust motes all follow one temperature model. Move your cursor along the coil and the nearest turns flare and spit sparks. On a phone, touch does the same.

Craft floor: pause control (WCAG 2.2.2); reduced-motion visitors get the filament steady at warm amber — a designed still, not a blank. One canvas, compositor-only DOM, capped device-pixel-ratio. Whole page with three embedded brand fonts: ~105KB, zero libraries.
