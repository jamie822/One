Rationale for the flagship lock: the founder singled out the v1 hero's drama
as the thing to beat, and Tungsten is that drama rebuilt with discipline:
dark plus warm light is the visual grammar of "we answer at 9pm", it needs
zero photography, and it contains none of the 2024 slop cluster (aurora, dot
grid, power core, tilt, magnetic, cyan glow). Solar and EV get the
energy-flow moment inside the direction: amber dots on offset-path, panel to
battery to home, drawn in brand line style on the lit paper sections.
